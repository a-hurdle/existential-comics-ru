Title: "Pyrrho wins the lottery"


Pane 1:

Pyrrho stands in front of a lottery machine.

Pyrrho (P): Three of three, not bad!


Pane 2:

P: Four of four! Just need one more!


Pane 3:

P: Come on NINE!

P: Come on come on come on come on commme oooonnn!!!


Pane 4:

The 9 ball comes up.


Pane 5:

P: YES!!! I can't believe I won!


Pane 6:

Pyrrho looks puzzled and sad.


Hover text: Pyrrho sometimes has a hard time believing whether or not
it is butter


Description:

Pyrrho was an ancient philosopher and one of the first
skeptics. Little is known of Pyrhho, since most of his work was lost,
however he was thought to be very extreme in his skepticism; perhaps
even making the claim that we should hold no beliefs, as nothing can
be truly known.

Find out more on the History of Philosophy With Any Gaps episode
<http://www.historyofphilosophy.net/pyrrho> or at the Stanford
Enclyclopia of Philosophy <http://plato.stanford.edu/entries/pyrrho/>
