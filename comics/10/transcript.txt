Title: Logicians Find a Genie


Pane 1 (genie.jpg):

Logician 1 (L1): Hey look at that! It looks like a magic lamp!


Pane 2:

Genie (G): I am the all powerful genie! I will grant you any three
wishes.

The logicians: WHOA!


Pane 3:

L1: Anything?

G: Yes!

L1: And you are truly all powerful?

G: Indeed!

L1: In that case, my first wish is for you to make an object so big
that even you can't move it!


Pane 4:

Logician 2, likely Rudolf Carnap (L2): Pst pst

L1: Hehe... Good one...

L1: For my next wish, I wish to own everything which I didn't acquire
via a wish!


Pane 5:

L1: And for my last wish, I wish for none of the wishes to have their
effect, including this one!

G: Well, since you wasted your wishes on logical impossibilities, I'm
going back in the lamp for the next 1000 years!


Pane 6:

L1: Nice! We nailed him!

L2: Totally!


Pane 7:

L2: We probably should have wished for a million dollars or
something...

L1: Yeah, or at least some water.

L2: Well, we definitely should have wished to get out of this desert.

L1: Yeah... We are probably going to die out here now.


Pane 8:

L1: You think he would have let us wish for more wishes?

L2: I think the trick is to wish for more genies...


Hover text: "Or maybe you wish for the power to wish for more wishes first. Hey is that a lake over there?" "No, it's probably just your heat stroked brain hallucinating"
